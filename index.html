Text extraction and system speech happen locally via pdf.js and the Web Speech API. The optional neural voice (Kokoro, open-source, Apache-2.0) also runs entirely on-device via WebAssembly — its one-time model download is cached by your browser, and no text ever leaves your device either way.
Works best on text-based PDFs — scanned/image-only pages can't be read aloud.
